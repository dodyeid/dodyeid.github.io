On the matter of control

Here is a thing the Church has understood for two thousand years and meteorologists have understood for about forty: you cannot control the weather. Jeremiah didn’t know what the weekend looked like. The disciples didn’t check AccuWeather before setting out on the Sea of Galilee. And yet — somehow — the days that mattered still happened. The wine ran out at Cana and someone handled it.

There is a particular kind of faith, the kind practiced by brides and farmers and sailors, that says: I have done everything I can. The rest is not mine to hold. It is, arguably, the whole point.

Also, it’s June in Cleveland, and the forecast is actually quite good. But we’ll get to that.
